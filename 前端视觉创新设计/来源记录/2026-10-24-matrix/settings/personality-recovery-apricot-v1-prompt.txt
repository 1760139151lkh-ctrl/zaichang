Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: a restrained recovery-state detail design for the existing Chinese desktop app 在场, landscape 1536 by 1024.
Primary request: the person was carefully editing how a companion speaks and addresses them; another saved edit arrives. Keep their own draft visibly in place and let them look at the current saved words before deciding. Express care through continuity and a clear choice, without a bureaucratic warning.
Scene: one single app screen, same warm ivory paper, muted gold, delicate outer window light, quiet daytime wooden room. No human figure or avatar. Existing unobtrusive top navigation 聊天 今天 留下 and settings tabs 陪伴的样子 / 怎么相处 / 阅读与节奏 / 连接与资料, with 怎么相处 active. Do not invent a dashboard.
Main layout follows a familiar writing-left, listening-right composition. Heading 你喜欢的相处方式. Left writing area: name field 丰川祥子; a large comfortable textarea labeled 你希望怎样相处 holding the person's own draft 讲题时，把理由讲清楚；我想说心事时，先听完。 Beneath it, a compact unchanged nickname field 如何称呼你 小灰灰. The caret is still inside the same textarea, showing continuity, not a new empty editor. Existing expandable row 再写细一点 stays below. Normal footer action 留下这些约定 is visibly unavailable until the competing saved edit has been reviewed; secondary 先不改了 remains quiet.
Right area remains the existing 试着这样说 example, with scenario 心情低落 and a short personal reply in a small soft paper quotation. It must not dominate the page.
Recovery detail: directly below the left editing area, one small folded-paper bookmark integrated into the same sheet, not an alert banner or big modal. Exact title 另一处也改过这里。 The source's current saved words are expanded read-only in an inset below a discreet link 收起另一份. Label 现在留下的文字. Name 丰川祥子. A short saved description 讲题时直接一点；谈心时，把话听完再一起想。 A saved nickname field 如何称呼你 小灰. Contrast draft and saved words using placement, tiny quotation marks and paper edges, not red/green diff colors. Do not strike through or dim the person's draft.
At the foot of this small bookmark, two precise actions: a muted-gold button 用这份草稿替换 and a quiet text action 继续改草稿. They are not automatic merge controls. The person's own draft and old nickname remain untouched while reading the inset. No further instructional paragraph; the visible two texts make the difference understandable.
Style: breathable, readable Chinese type, minimal separators, real form controls, stable shared alignment with the existing editor. Soft paper, no piles of small cards, no bright green UI, no red error icon, no technical fields or revision numbers, no progress, no safety checklist, no promotional warmth slogans. This is a design reference of a single state; do not render a side-by-side board of entire screens. Preserve human choice and the existing writing/examples relationship.
