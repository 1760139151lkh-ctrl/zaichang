Use case: scientific-educational, with product ui-mockup material direction.
Asset: ONE new product design concept for 在场, a quiet shared paper where a person can stop a grand-piano keystroke halfway and understand it. Start from text only. No input/reference images.

Draw a coherent, beautifully restrained 1536x1024 landscape scene: one warm ivory sheet lying naturally on a softly lit walnut desk by an unseen window. It belongs in a refined Chinese companion app, intimate and thoughtful, with the softness of printed Japanese music study notes and the clarity of Apple interfaces. No app window, sidebar, avatar, character or decorative dashboard. Warm ink, muted brass, soft blue, paper white; NO green UI or green felt.

The MAIN CONTENT is ONE large clear SIDE-VIEW SCHEMATIC of a grand piano action, paused in the EARLY DRIVEN PART of a keystroke, BEFORE escapement, at about 20% of the complete press/strike/release journey. It must visibly explain a single continuous physical chain: a long horizontal KEY has its FRONT on the LEFT depressed downward, a central balance-pin pivot supports the key, its rear on the RIGHT rises and lifts the capstan; the capstan lifts the hinged WIPPEN; the slender JACK still CONTACTS the underside of the hammer KNUCKLE and pushes it upward; the HAMMER pivots upward toward one horizontal STRING above it but DOES NOT YET TOUCH the string. The hammer has a distinct fixed pivot and one rigid shank, head not floating. The DAMPER has lifted clear ABOVE the string as the key is pressed. The damper and hammer are separate parts. Muted-red felt permitted, but no green. Every lever has one credible support; no arbitrary rods, disconnected joints, gear wheels or architectural-looking decoration. Prefer a truthful, legible two-dimensional articulated teaching schematic over photorealistic machinery. Fine wood edges and a touch of watercolor softness are enough. A very faint warm-gray ghost of the RESTING key and hammer may show the displacement, no overlapping duplicate mechanism.

Quiet Chinese title at upper left exactly: “把这一瞬，停在手边”
Small component labels with precise fine leaders, each ONCE: “琴键”, “联动器”, “顶杆”, “琴槌”, “琴弦”, “制音器”.
Below mechanism: one elegant process slider, handle near the first fifth, with only endpoint labels “按下” and “回落”. Immediately below, a modest two-octave keyboard with mechanically correct black-key repeating groups of TWO then THREE, a C key softly depressed, no fake extra black keys. Beside it a small warm-brass sustain pedal and one subtle actual-looking decaying waveform. One quiet action at bottom right exactly “接着这个变化聊 ↗”. No paragraph instructions, progress percentages, validation badges, cards within cards, arbitrary numbers or hard split backgrounds.

Show emotional purpose through the object: enough empty paper to breathe, the player's gesture has agency, the mechanism is patiently waiting where they paused. Make the motion and joint relationships carry the explanation. The illustration is only a design proposal; do not fabricate a measured piano model.
