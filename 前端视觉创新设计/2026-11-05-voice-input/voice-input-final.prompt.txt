Use case: ui-mockup. Final visual reference for 在场, a Windows companion app whose users prefer sparse icons and have reading difficulties. EXACTLY three close-up views of the SAME composer, vertically arranged, on warm lightly painted wood in a quiet window-lit room. No people. No headings, labels, annotations, notes, paragraphs, dialog, popover, download flow, progress ring, preferences, gear, language, microphone selection or model management. The model is already included in the software.
The only visible sentence in each composer is the user's editable draft “我想把这段证明再读一遍。”. Rounded dark warm brown translucent writing surface, quiet fine gold border, thin cream Lucide-like icons.
Top view, resting: plus icon left; draft center; small microphone icon and gold circular send arrow right. Only those three functional controls.
Middle view, recording: same exact composer; microphone becomes a small stop square with a subtle real-audio amplitude halo in its original slot; inside a single compact lower row, numeric elapsed “00:05” at left, a wide delicate amber waveform of changing sound levels, and ONE small X cancel at far right. Keep the editable draft and send arrow usable. No visible status words.
Bottom view, finished: original mic and send arrow restored; compact lower row contains a small check indicator at left and ONLY two icon actions at right, restore previous clipboard (clipboard with return arrow) and undo insertion (curved arrow). No close button, no caption, no settings or copy action unless the original clipboard could not be replaced. Leave breathing room around every necessary control. Every control must look precise and restrained; no generic dashboard, no fake progress or neon sound decoration.
Optical alignment, clear warm cream ink, thin 1.6px icon strokes, muted gold, no green UI. The room is just background atmosphere. Frame only the three cropped input views at realistic 580px-wide proportions, plenty of quiet space. This is a visual specification for actual reactive UI, with a very real sense of sound shaping the waveform and a button morphing immediately when touched. Do not add any feature not explicitly described.
